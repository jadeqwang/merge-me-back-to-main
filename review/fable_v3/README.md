# Merge: the Fable cut, v3 (third full cut)

`merge_fable_v3_720p.mp4`: the whole song (4:42), 720p, compressed to fit under GitHub's 100 MB limit, so fine detail (thin roads,
small text, crowds) is softer than the real 1080p render.

What changed since v2 (the notes worked from are `fable/NOTES_v2.md`):
- 0:28 "I split myself": whole bodies at a low three-quarter angle, and the first split is a Banach-Tarski wink quoted from "Rewind":
  she is scored into triangles that lift as rigid pieces and seat as two whole bodies. The reverse happens at 1:27 on "Fold me in again".
  The road under her feet reads `split: 5 pieces, nothing left over` and `merge: banach-tarski, inverted`.
- 1:04 "from" is no longer upside down; 1:30-1:37 has a push-in with her road's commits locking on toward her
- 1:48-1:58 the float comes home with the pulses; the stiff sit-up is covered
- 2:40-3:01 the troupe runs into the letters on real run cycles; MAIN lands on a gold-rose mirror; the camera comes down to the front
  for the rise and the arms flung wide; the troupe stays behind her, lit gold, to the salute
- 3:36-3:42 the returning comets converge; 3:48 is a waist-up of her holding the light against the sun
- 4:02 crouch covered; 4:18 map labels larger

Still not right:
- 2:44-2:46: before the fall the letters are cropped and the standing bodies read as dashes from above
- 4:16: runners are specks on white just before the map
- 3:56.6: the handover into the day section is covered by a cut to her feet rather than fixed
- she has no face and mitten hands (an anime-styled figure with a face is being tried separately)

Every pixel is drawn in code; nothing was filmed or generated; the martial arts is retimed motion capture (CMU Graphics Lab Motion
