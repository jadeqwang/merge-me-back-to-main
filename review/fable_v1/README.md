# Merge: the Fable cut, v1 (rough first assembly)

`merge_fable_v1_720p.mp4`: the whole song (4:42), 720p, compressed to fit under GitHub's 100 MB limit, so fine detail is softer than the real render.

This is the FIRST full cut with the real figure: structure, performances and timing are in, composition is not. It is here so you can see
the direction, not as a finished pass. Every pixel is drawn in code; nothing was filmed or generated; the martial arts is retimed motion capture
(CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, ACCAD).

Known problems (already being reworked, act by act; the full list is `fable/NOTES_v1.md`):
- she is too small in the verses, chorus 1 and the day outro
- a white-hot horizon band overpowers verse 1 (0:34-1:00)
- the frame empties after the first fork (0:06-0:14)
- 2:20-2:26 is a flat blue top-down with specks for figures
- the violet branch's handwriting (3:16-3:21) appears beside her hand instead of coming from the pencil
- HUD and terminal text are too small to read

What to look at: the tree to 1,024 (0:14), her hair becoming the roads (1:38), the butterfly kick in the sun (2:33), the fallen troupe
spelling MAIN (2:46), the violet branch writing and sitting down (3:16-3:28), the catch at sunrise (3:50), the whole-film graph (4:18).
