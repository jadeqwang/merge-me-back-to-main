# Merge: the Fable cut, v4 (fourth full cut, anime figure)

`merge_fable_v4_720p.mp4`: the whole song (4:42), 720p, compressed to fit under GitHub's 100 MB limit, so fine detail (thin roads,
small text, crowds, faces at a distance) is softer than the real 1080p render.

What changed since v3 (notes worked from: `fable/NOTES_v3.md`):
- She has a face. The protagonist is now an anime-styled figure (eyes, expressions, gaze, blinks, a neck, a centre parting), tuned
  toward Jade. At night her face is calm and in shadow; against the sun she is a silhouette; her first fully lit, open-eyed look is at
  the catch ("Every life I've lived I have become", 3:50); by day she is joyful. `--q "fig=classic"` still renders the old figure.
- 0:26 a comet runs up her road during the fall from the tree; 1:00-1:06 "Where I started from" stays put and upright
- the Banach-Tarski lattice (0:28, 1:27) is shaped to her body and rides her joints
- 2:42-2:47 the troupe arrives under a high oblique that holds the whole word MAIN; overhead exactly on the fall; the salute is gold
- 3:15-3:22 the back of her head has form (rim, strands, bun) and her profile shows as she writes
- 3:56 the handover into the day is a real blended performance; 4:12-4:17 people stay readable until the pull-up to the map

Still not right:
- nothing has been watched as video by its makers, only as dense frame strips: foot slides and small pops may remain
  (known: back foot slides ~30 cm at 3:56.5-3:57; arms dip then rise at 3:57.3-3:57.7)
- 2:42-2:44 the oblique is dim; the kick apex at 2:57.4 is a dark shape
- 3:08-3:15 the violet branch's look east is seen only from behind
- hands are mittens with drawn finger lines

Every pixel is drawn in code; nothing was filmed or generated; the martial arts is retimed motion capture (CMU Graphics Lab Motion
Capture Database, ACCAD).
