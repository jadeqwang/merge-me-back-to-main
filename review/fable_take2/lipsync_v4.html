Lip-sync picker: the two drawn sung shots

Press play on a shot. It loops. Drag the slider (or use -20 / +20) until the mouth lands on the words, the way you picked +80 ms on take D. A positive number means the voice comes later than the picture. Each slider starts on the value I measured. Then send back the line in the green box. Wired speakers or headphones are best: Bluetooth adds its own delay.

Send back:

The picture is the plate's own frames (24 fps), exactly what the film uses; the sound is the song, played through Web Audio. The picture follows the sound's clock (the moment you hear, corrected for the sound card's delay), so the number on the slider is the true offset. "As cut" loops the part of the clip the film shows, with a short lead-in; "whole clip" loops all five seconds. Media is read from ../../work_fable/take2/lipsync_v4/ (not in the repo).
