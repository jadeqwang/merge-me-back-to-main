What changed in v11

  • 3:22-3:27: she comes apart into the letters of this song (its lines in order, first lines leave first, Latin only); the small letters are larger so they read at phone size.
  • 3:28: the streak is a comet whose tail is the filename HANDOFF.md, once: a bright head, the letters in its glowing tail, the far letters dissolving into light. Same timing (enters on the "Merge" hit, at the galaxy's core on the next bar); it comes again from her side at 3:31.
  • 3:39-3:47: what pours into her hands and runs up her is Merge's own lines (violet); she is written in words from your songs, and each of the twelve lives arrives as a phrase from one of your songs in its branch colour.
  • 3:48-3:51: the letters gather into MERGE ME / BACK TO / MAIN, then FOLD / ME IN, MAIN, ME, and the last mark is a violet I that sinks in on "become"; daylight opens from her chest as torn paper to the 3:55 impact.
  • No slab lyrics 3:39-3:58 (the subtitle files still carry every line).
  • A notation per life: the ten life introductions (0:47-0:55) and the twelve-panel grid (0:56-1:02) are written in each life's own notation (staff, hanzi, A T G C, IPA, your lines, Greek and maths, A C G U, code, a child's capitals).
  • Glyph size as the merge variable: largest at 0:29, finer through chorus 1 and the float, finest in the catch, clean line in daylight.
  • 4:29: the car box starts typing on the cut, so the finished line stays up about a second before it goes at 4:32.5.
  • Every v11 change can be switched off for comparison (?v11=0 renders the v10 cut).
