Merge, film v1: timestamped notes and decisions

Everything you type is kept in this browser (it survives a reload). When you are done, press Copy everything at the bottom of the right column and paste it to Fable, or download it as a file.

Video

Keys (when not typing): N add a note at the current time, space play or pause, ← → one second, , . one frame, Esc leave a text box. Adding a note pauses the film and puts the cursor in the note. Note times are song times, whichever file is playing. The section files have more detail than the whole film but their times are approximate to a few frames.
