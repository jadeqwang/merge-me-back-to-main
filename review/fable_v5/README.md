# Merge: the Fable cut, v5 (fifth full cut)

`merge_fable_v5_720p.mp4`: the whole song (4:42), 720p, compressed to fit under GitHub's 100 MB limit, so fine detail (thin roads,
small text, crowds, faces at a distance) is softer than the real 1080p render.

What changed since v4 (from a frame-by-frame motion review; notes worked from: `fable/NOTES_v4.md`):
- 3:49-3:56 "Every life I've lived I have become": the rigid T-pose is replaced by a living gesture (arms open in a soft, uneven V,
  weight on one leg, still opening through the hold); the rise (1:58) and arms wide (2:52) got the same treatment
- 1:56.6 the float ends before she moves; her lift out of the water is seen from the water line
- 2:05.3 and other fast drops: her hair no longer stands up in spikes
- 3:56.6 into the day: her back foot steps instead of sliding
- 3:45-3:48 she is larger in the high shot after the catch; in the 1,024-copy shots a pool of orange light marks her
- 4:24-4:42 terminal: she breathes, shifts her weight and turns her head once; credits clear of the road text
- lyrics kept inside a 5% margin ("and wander" was cut by the edge at 1:44); small framing fixes in the lives, the salute, the
  writing close-up

Still not right:
- hands are mittens with drawn finger lines, most visible in the open-arms hold (3:52-3:56)
- 2:14.6-2:19 a few bodies still cross the frame edges in the side truck
- 3:25 faceted trouser folds as the violet branch sits down
- its makers have reviewed it as dense frame strips, never as playing video

Every pixel is drawn in code; nothing was filmed or generated; the martial arts is retimed motion capture (CMU Graphics Lab Motion
Capture Database, ACCAD).
