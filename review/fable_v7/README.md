# Merge: the Fable cut, v7

`merge_fable_v7_720p.mp4`: the whole song (4:42), 720p, compressed to fit under GitHub's 100 MB limit, so fine detail is softer than
the real render. This is the code-drawn cut the masters are made from.

What changed since v6 (notes: `fable/notes/final_fixes.md`):
- 3:15-3:22 the writing close-up uses the full-detail figure: smooth sleeve, a real pencil grip, round bun
- 2:59-3:01 the salute is frontal and symmetric, she is on the centre line with the 1,023 in a wedge behind her
- 2:57.5 the kick apex reads against the sky

`seedance_test/`: three short silent clips cut from this version (`in_write.mp4` 3:16-3:22, `in_become.mp4` 3:50-3:56.5,
`in_day.mp4` 3:56.65-4:02.65). They are the input for a video-model test Jade asked for (redrawing the character from her character
sheet with the code render as the motion reference); the model needs its input video at a public URL.

Every pixel of the cut is drawn in code; nothing was filmed or generated; the martial arts is retimed motion capture (CMU Graphics Lab
Motion Capture Database, ACCAD).
