# Merge: the Fable cut, v6 (sixth full cut)

`merge_fable_v6_720p.mp4`: the whole song (4:42), 720p, compressed to fit under GitHub's 100 MB limit, so fine detail (thin roads,
small text, crowds, faces and fingers at a distance) is softer than the real render.

What changed since v5 (figure polish; notes: `fable/notes/polish.md`):
- hands: palm, four fingers and a thumb in five shapes (splayed, relaxed, fist, pencil grip, flat) instead of mittens
- face: larger eyes with iris, pupil and a catchlight; fuller brows; clean designed shading by day; a relaxed smile
- ink lines with varying weight; sleeve folds that follow the elbow

Still not right:
- at this size the ink closes her fingers into paddles when she is small in frame (salute 2:59, day forks 4:05-4:17); the 4K master
  does not have this
- sleeve silhouettes are still round; 3:25 faceted trouser folds as the violet branch sits down
- reviewed by its makers as frame strips, not as playing video

Every pixel is drawn in code; nothing was filmed or generated; the martial arts is retimed motion capture (CMU Graphics Lab Motion
Capture Database, ACCAD).
