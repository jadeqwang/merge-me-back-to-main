# Merge: the Fable cut, v2 (second full cut)

`merge_fable_v2_720p.mp4`: The whole song (4:42), compressed to fit under GitHub's 100 MB limit, so fine detail (thin roads, small text, crowds) is softer than
the real 1080p render.

Every act was reworked from the v1 notes: she is large in frame almost everywhere, the lives are one-bar vignettes with props, chorus 1
is the braid of roads into main, the build peels copies out of her bar by bar, the drop is the butterfly kick in the sun and the
silhouettes against it, the violet branch writes `docs: start here` from the pencil tip, and the film ends in a terminal on the repo's
real log. Every pixel is drawn in code; nothing was filmed or generated; the martial arts is retimed motion capture (CMU Graphics Lab
Motion Capture Database, ACCAD).
