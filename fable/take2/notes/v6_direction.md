## The idea of v6

**The violet branch (`jade/rare-earth`) is the narrator.** She is the one who will not merge. The question the film asks from the
start is whether what she learned gets home. What gets home is not memory: main Jade gets it back as intuition, as what her hands
and feet know. One object carries it: **her pencil and the page.**

1. She is planted from the first seconds and seen not turning, not running.
2. **The drop is her drawing.** The pencil crowds (orchestra, wushu, MAIN) are what she draws on the page at the dish: a branch that
   will never see main, drawing what merging must be like, gold on black paper, at night. Chorus 2 in daylight and full colour is
   the real one. (Jade: "Maybe the reason the orchestra and wushu agent swarms are drawn in pencil is because she's drawing them?")
3. The page leaves the dish, crosses the film as a light, and arrives in the frost.
4. After the credits, one shot in a car: the camera is the person she is talking to.
