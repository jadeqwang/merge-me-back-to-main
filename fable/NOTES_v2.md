# Director's notes on v2 (second full cut, 1080p)

v2: `work_fable/out/v2.mp4` (1920x1080, 30 fps, 8,475 frames, rendered in 505 s), sheets `work_fable/stills/v2/sheet_1..3.jpg`
(one frame per 2 s, timestamps burnt in). Review copy for Jade: `review/fable_v2/`.

v2 is a different film from v1: she is large in almost every shot, the lives read, chorus 1 is rich, the build and the silhouettes
against the sun are the strongest minutes, the writing shot (3:16-3:22) and the terminal ending work. The list below is what still
falls short. SPEC section 2 stays binding. Everything so far was judged on stills: **every pass this round must also check motion**
(cut dense strips, 8-10 frames per second, of each shot you touch and of every cut point in your act; look for pose pops, skating,
frozen holds, type crossing a body).

## Act A (seq 01-04, 0:00-1:10)
- 0:10-0:13: the four walkers are ~10% of frame height before the cut to the tree. Crane less, or cut to the tree a beat earlier
  on a measured time; they should still read as people.
- 0:26-0:28: the tree leaves frame and we look at bare streaks, then a half second of mirror. Carry the eye: the camera should fall
  from the tree toward one tip and find her running on it by 0:28.0.
- 0:28-0:34 "I split myself a thousand ways": high behind-the-back angle makes the runners crouched lumps, cut by the bottom edge.
  Lower three-quarter angle (front or rear), whole bodies in frame, the split on "split" (28.62) legible as one body becoming two
  stepping apart, four on "thousand" (30.29). The big counter must not sit on a road or a body.
- 0:43-0:45: the orb is small; give it a soft halo about the size of her head and let it light her chest and face-side as it leaves.
- 0:56: `home` she is ~30% of frame; come in until she is half the frame and the orrery still fits.
- 1:04: the word "from" is drawn upside down / mirrored for about two seconds (the lyric is laid on the ground and the camera has
  passed over it). Fix: the line must be upright to this camera for its whole time on screen.
- Sandstorm and wushu lives appear only in the roll call: acceptable, leave.

## Act B (seq 05-07, 1:10-2:05)
- 1:10-1:15: good. Check in motion that the sprint does not skate against the ground text.
- 1:29.5-1:37 "Nothing that I lived can come undone": 8 s on one static frame with a small body. Keep the framing idea (her road
  running back, nodes locking on) but give it a slow push-in of the camera (end at her about 55% of frame height), and make the
  node-locking travel toward us from the horizon, the last node locking under her feet on "undone" (final syllable).
- 1:48-1:55: she shrinks to a few percent under a wall of text. Hold the widest point for at most two bars, then descend back toward
  her with the pulses ("Merge and return": the pulses come home, the camera comes home with them), so 1:55 hands over to the sit-up
  already close.
- 1:56-1:58 the sit-up: reads as a pale mannequin doing a plank; the trousers bunch. Shorten what we see of it: stay on her face-side
  three-quarter from the water line and cut to the rise on its measured time, or cover it with the low camera rising with her.
- 2:03.5-2:05.3: she is lost in the glare. Pull the burst's exposure down around her so her silhouette is intact until the cut.

## Act C (seq 08-10, 2:05-3:01)
- 2:05-2:40: the best of the film. Leave the shots; motion check only (run-pose pops at 2:40 and at arrivals).
- 2:44-2:53 MAIN: ten seconds top-down on flat salmon, and the rise (b98-99) and the arms-wide hold (b100, 172.300) happen where
  nobody can read them. New shape:
  - b97.1 (167.082) the fall, top-down, MAIN lands. Hold about one bar. Ground is the gold-rose mirror with the sun's glitter path,
    bodies dark with their lane accents; not a flat colour.
  - during b98 the camera comes down and round to a low front oblique (sun behind them or three-quarter) so that the rise at b99.1
    and the arms flung wide on 172.300 are seen from the front, rows of bodies receding, hero in front and large.
  - b101-103: the troupe stays in frame behind the hero (v2 shows her alone).
- The hero must be part of the word (v2: she lies 6.5 m east of it). Put her in a letter cell (the apex of the A, or the dot of
  the I's top bar), marked only by her main-orange accents.
- The runs into the letter cells: use `run_from_stand` / `run_to_stand` (M3); only ~500 of 1,023 can reach their cell at run speed
  by b96.1, so restage: assign cells by nearest free cell (minimum travel), let the far ones leave earlier (staggered starts read as
  a wave), cap speed at sprint (4.93 m/s), and everyone arrives facing east within 20 degrees.
- 2:58-3:01 salute: troupe is cream on magenta. Warm it (gold key from the sun side, cool rim), keep the sky from going flat.

## Act D (seq 11-13, 3:01-3:57)
- 3:16-3:22: keep exactly. (Her head is a clean black shape against the violet: that is the night rule, and it works.)
- 3:37-3:41: sparse. Other comets (the returning branches) should be visibly converging on the same point from both sides, with the
  horizon warming, so the frame fills as the line "becomes the one" lands.
- 3:41-3:42.7: hold the sun under the horizon until the 222.735 hit, then let it break.
- 3:48.4-3:49.7: a near-empty wide with her tiny at the bottom. Replace with a closer transitional frame (the violet light arriving
  at her chest, waist-up), so 3:50 follows from it.
- 3:56.6: check the handover into seq 14 in motion (light step, pose pop).

## Act E (seq 14-16, 3:57-4:42)
- 4:02: the deep crouch is a ball of trouser. Re-time the shot so the crouch is seen for a few frames only (cut or whip on the
  leap), or shoot it from the front-quarter where the legs separate.
- 4:00-4:04: the half turn; check the pivot foot in motion.
- 4:09-4:12: the children at full stride: stride scaled to their size.
- 4:14-4:19: the fan is thin lines and specks, then a frame with no subject. Stay lower and closer for longer (runners readable as
  people for the whole "Back to main"), and reach the map with a continuous pull-up that keeps main-orange under the centre.
- 4:18-4:24 map: labels are still small in the sheet: every label 26 px or more at 1080p, none crossed by an arterial; the map's
  cream should not be a flat card: keep a little of the mirror's gradient and the roads' glow.
- 4:26-4:38 terminal: good. Keep.
